Internal build — carries the `-dev` suffix; the update channel it belongs to is
`dev`.

**Two themes, one app: EVA 01 and EVA 00.** A new plugin, `@muen/dsh-eva-theme`,
adds a **Theme** row to Settings → General with a card per theme. EVA 01 is the
dark one — Unit-01 purple and mint on a near-black field. EVA 00 is the light one,
teal and navy on white. Picking either repaints the whole app immediately, and the
choice survives a restart.

**A real light theme, not a dimmed dark one.** EVA 00 switches the app to light
mode rather than tinting the dark palette, so text, borders, code blocks and
floating panels are all solved for a light background. That is the reason the
theme engine underneath is the one a working community theme uses, rather than one
written here: it already treats light as a first-class case instead of a variant
of dark.

**Nothing else about the app changed.** The sidebar, the loading screen and the
Settings layout are exactly as they were in 0.1.6-dev.

Wraps DeepSeek Harness 0.1.5-rc.1 — unchanged, including the DeepSeek 4.1 model
adapter.

Signed with Developer ID, notarized by Apple, stapled. Gates: `pnpm verify:release`,
`pnpm verify:surfaces`, and `pnpm verify:mount` — which boots a real window and
reads the Theme row back out of Settings → General, so "present but not mounted"
cannot pass as success.

Install: download `Mitsumeru-0.1.7-dev-arm64.dmg`. An installed 0.1.6-dev offers
this version by itself.
