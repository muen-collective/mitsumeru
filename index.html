Mitsumeru

A local-first AI workspace built on the Mitsu harness. Sessions, workspaces, and keys stay on your machine — every surface is a plugin.

v0.1.2 · unsigned preview
macOS — Apple Silicon mitsumeru-mac-arm64.dmg Production build macOS — Intel mitsumeru-dev-mac-x64.dmg Dev build (0.1.1) Windows x64 mitsumeru-dev-windows-x64-setup.exe Dev build (0.1.1)

Preview builds are unsigned. On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open on first launch. On Windows, allow through SmartScreen. The Intel and Windows builds are dev-channel builds until they are rebuilt from their native platforms.

Built to stay on your machine

Mitsumeru packages the Mitsu harness as a native desktop app. Your data has its own home — separate from everything else.

Local-first

Workspaces, sessions, and credentials live under your user data directory. The harness runs on a private 127.0.0.1 port.

Every surface is a plugin

Settings, rail, docs, starter pack, and brand are @muen/mitsu-* plugins, seeded into your profile on first launch.

One identity

A separate app home (com.muen.mitsumeru.app) means Mitsumeru never shares data with — or masquerades as — another product.

Open source

MIT-licensed, forked from DSH Desktop, built on the DeepSeek Harness runtime. Contribute on GitHub.

Requirements — macOS 13+ (Apple Silicon or Intel) · Windows 10/11 x64 · ~1 GB free disk
