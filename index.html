Mitsumeru

A local-first AI workspace built on the Mitsu harness. Sessions, workspaces, and keys stay on your machine.

Download for macOS

Built to stay on your machine

Mitsumeru packages the Mitsu harness as a native desktop app. Your data has its own home — separate from everything else.

Local-first

Workspaces, sessions, and credentials live under your user data directory. The harness runs on a private 127.0.0.1 port.

Every surface is a plugin

Settings, rail, docs, starter pack, and brand are @muen/mitsu-* plugins, seeded into your profile on first launch.

One identity

A separate app home (com.muen.mitsumeru.app) means Mitsumeru never shares data with — or masquerades as — another product.

Open source

MIT-licensed, forked from DSH Desktop, built on the DeepSeek Harness runtime. Contribute on GitHub.

Requirements — macOS 13+ (Apple Silicon or Intel) · ~1 GB free disk
